## FORMAT FOR PREPARATION OF PROJECT REPORT

### FOR

**B.E. / B. TECH. / B. ARCH.**

---

# 1. Arrangement of Contents

The sequence in which the project report material should be arranged and bound is as follows:

1. Cover Page & Title Page
2. Bonafide Certificate
3. Abstract
4. Table of Contents
5. List of Tables
6. List of Figures
7. List of Symbols, Abbreviations and Nomenclature
8. Chapters
9. Appendices
10. References

> Tables and figures shall be introduced in the appropriate places.

---

# 2. Page Dimension and Binding Specifications

* The dimension of the project report should be **A4 size**.
* The project report should be **bound using a flexible cover of thick white art paper**.
* The cover should be **printed in black letters** and the **text for printing should be identical**.

---

# 3. Preparation Format

## 3.1 Cover Page & Title Page

A specimen copy of the Cover Page & Title Page of the project report is given in **Appendix 1**.

---

## 3.2 Bonafide Certificate

* The Bonafide Certificate shall be in **double line spacing**.
* **Font Style:** Times New Roman
* **Font Size:** 14

The certificate shall carry:

* The **supervisor’s signature**
* The **supervisor’s name**
* **Academic designation** *(not administrative responsibilities)*
* **Department**
* **Full address of the institution**

The term **“SUPERVISOR”** must be typed in **capital letters** between the supervisor’s name and academic designation.

---

## 3.3 Abstract

The Abstract should be:

* A **one-page synopsis** of the project report
* **Double line spacing**
* **Font Style:** Times New Roman
* **Font Size:** 14

---

## 3.4 Table of Contents

The Table of Contents should list:

* All material **following it**
* Any material **preceding it**

However:

* **Title Page** and **Bonafide Certificate** will **not appear in the contents list**
* Their **page numbers will be in lower-case Roman numerals**

Formatting:

* **1.5 line spacing**

A specimen is provided in **Appendix 3**.

---

## 3.5 List of Tables

* Use the **exact captions** as they appear **above the tables in the text**
* **1.5 line spacing**

---

## 3.6 List of Figures

* Use the **exact captions** as they appear **below the figures**
* **1.5 line spacing**

---

## 3.7 List of Symbols, Abbreviations and Nomenclature

* **1.5 line spacing**
* Use **standard symbols and abbreviations**

---

## 3.8 Chapters

The chapters may be broadly divided into three parts:

1. **Introductory Chapter**
2. **Chapters developing the main theme of the project**
3. **Conclusion**

### Guidelines

* Each chapter should have an **appropriate title**
* Tables and figures should appear **near the reference where cited**
* **Footnotes should be used sparingly**

Footnotes:

* **Single spacing**
* Placed **directly below on the same page**

---

## 3.9 Appendices

Appendices provide **supplementary information** that may otherwise distract from the main text.

### Rules

* Number using **Arabic numerals**

Example:

```
Appendix 1
Appendix 2
```

* Tables and references inside appendices should be **numbered similarly to chapters**
* Appendices should carry the **title of the work**
* The same title should appear in the **contents page**

---

## 3.10 List of References

References should be:

* Typed **4 spaces below the heading “REFERENCES”**
* In **alphabetical order**
* **Single spacing**
* **Left-justified**

The reference list should be ordered by the **first author's name**.

### Example

#### REFERENCES

1. Ariponnammal, S. and Natarajan, S. (1994)
   *Transport Phonomena of Sm Sel – X Asx*,
   **Pramana – Journal of Physics**, Vol.42, No.1, pp.421–425.

2. Barnard, R.W. and Kellogg, C. (1980)
   *Applications of Convolution Operators to Problems in Univalent Function Theory*,
   **Michigan Math Journal**, Vol.27, pp.81–94.

3. Shin, K.G. and McKay, N.D. (1984)
   *Open Loop Minimum Time Control of Mechanical Manipulations and its Applications*,
   **Proceedings of American Control Conference**, San Diego, CA, pp.1231–1236.

---

### 3.10.1 Tables and Figures

* **Tables:** Tabulated numerical data in the body of the project report or appendices.

* **Figures:**
  All other visual material such as:

  * Charts
  * Graphs
  * Maps
  * Photographs
  * Diagrams

---

* The typed copies should have **black coloured text**.
* **1.5 line spacing** should be used for general text.
